游戏规则

1. 你是谁，你要做什么

你代表一个三体文明，和另一个文明连续相遇若干轮。目标是总分比对方高。你只需要做两件事：

  1. 开局前，选一个大脑电路出战。对局中它会自动替你行动。
  2. 对局中被打击时，决定要不要花 1 分读心。

2. 四个大脑电路

每轮电路的输入是对方上一轮的动作，输出是你本轮的动作。第 1 轮默认对方交流过。电路相同输入一定得到相同输出，它自己永远不会撒谎。

这 4 个电路已经在 X Layer 上通过 TapeOut 流片（TapeID 1.2.271～4.2.271）。对局中每一次计算、每一次读心，都是在链上调用电路的 eval 算出来的，任何人都可以复算。

电路对方上轮交流对方上轮打击性格
#1 执剑人交流打击你不动我不动，你打我必还手
#2 逆行者打击交流反着来
#3 拯救派交流交流永远相信善意
#4 清理者打击打击见光就打

3. 每轮怎么计分

一句话：一起交流双赢，互相打击双输，偷袭的人占便宜，被偷袭的人吃亏。

情况你对方
都交流33
你打击，对方交流50
你交流，对方打击05
都打击11

单看一轮，打击总能多拿分；可如果大家都打击，所有人都只剩 1 分。这就是黑暗森林。

4. 纪元

开局时选择纪元，它决定这一局的轮数、智子干扰率，以及能不能读心。

纪元轮数干扰率读心打法
恒纪元105%可以打击多半是真心的，读心常常白花钱
乱纪元1040%可以打击多半是误会，值得读心
三日凌空630%禁止误会无法澄清，全靠大脑本身
飞星纪元1020%可以干扰不多不少，打击是真是假最难判断
未知纪元10随机可以从恒纪元、乱纪元里随机抽一个，赛后公开

开局可以勾选“把本局规则的哈希承诺写上 X Layer”：开打前用钱包发一笔 0 OKB 的交易，把干扰计划、真实纪元和对手大脑的哈希写上链。赛后公开原文，点结果页的“链上验证”就能确认本局规则在开局前已定好、对局中没被改过。

5. 智子干扰

每轮每一方都有一定概率（由纪元决定的干扰率）被智子操控，大脑临时换成 #4 清理者，打出一次并非本意的打击。干扰对双方是对称的，你和对方每轮各自独立抽签。

  • 你自己被干扰时，界面会当场提示你：你知道自己的电路，本来就能推断出来。你没法替自己辩解，只能等对方读心还你清白。
  • 对方有没有被干扰，你看不到，赛后才公开。

6. 读心

被打击时，你可以花 1 分读心：系统用对方原本的大脑重新计算这一轮。

开局勾选“读心时用钱包烧 OKB”后，每次读心还要用钱包在 X Layer 上发一笔交易，把 0.0001 OKB 打进黑洞地址 0x…dEaD。这笔 OKB 无论读心结果如何都不退还，交易确认后才会读心，对局记录里可以点开每一笔读心交易。它代表“透明”的真实代价：将来正式版里，双方开局押 OKB，赢家拿走奖池，读心费就是博弈成本的一部分。

  • 算出来是交流，说明是智子干扰：对方这一轮改回交流，按真实动作计分，读心费退还。
  • 算出来还是打击，说明对方本性如此：扣你 1 分，比分不变。

对方的大脑在对局中保密，所以你要猜：这次打击是智子干的，还是遇到了清理者？

1. Who you are and what you do

You represent a civilization and meet another one for several rounds in a row. The goal is a higher total score than the opponent. You only make two kinds of decisions:

  1. Before the match, pick a brain circuit. During the match it plays for you automatically.
  2. When you are struck, decide whether to spend 1 point on mind-reading.

2. The four brain circuits

Each round, the circuit’s input is the opponent’s last move and its output is your move. In round 1 the opponent is assumed to have cooperated. The same input always gives the same output: the circuit itself never lies.

These 4 circuits were taped out on X Layer with TapeOut (TapeID 1.2.271 to 4.2.271). Every computation and every mind-read in a match is an on-chain call to the circuit’s eval, which anyone can recompute.

CircuitOpponent cooperatedOpponent struckPersonality
#1 SwordholderCooperateStrikeCalm while you are calm, always strikes back
#2 ContrarianStrikeCooperateDoes the opposite
#3 RedemptionistCooperateCooperateAlways believes in good faith
#4 CleanerStrikeStrikeStrikes at any light

3. Scoring each round

In short: cooperating together is a win-win, striking each other is a lose-lose, and a surprise strike exploits the cooperator.

SituationYouOpponent
Both cooperate33
You strike, opponent cooperates50
You cooperate, opponent strikes05
Both strike11

In a single round striking always scores more, but if everyone strikes, everyone gets only 1 point. That is the dark forest.

4. Eras

The era you pick decides the number of rounds, the sophon interference rate, and whether mind-reading is allowed.

EraRoundsInterferenceMind-readingHow to play
Stable Era105%YesStrikes are usually sincere; reading often wastes money
Chaotic Era1040%YesStrikes are usually misunderstandings; worth reading
Tri-Solar Day630%NoMisunderstandings cannot be cleared; it is all on your brain
Flying Star Era1020%YesModerate interference; real and fake strikes are hardest to tell apart
Unknown Era10RandomYesSecretly Stable or Chaotic, revealed after the match

You can tick “commit a hash of the match rules to X Layer”: before play, your wallet sends a 0 OKB transaction that puts a hash of the interference plan, the real era and the opponent brain on chain. The preimage is revealed afterwards, and “Verify on chain” on the result page confirms the rules were fixed before play and not changed during it.

5. Sophon interference

Each round, each side has a chance (the era’s interference rate) of being hijacked by the sophons: its brain is temporarily replaced by #4 Cleaner and strikes against its will. Interference is symmetric; you and the opponent are drawn independently each round.

  • When you are interfered with, the game tells you right away: you know your own circuit, so you could tell anyway. You cannot defend yourself; only the opponent’s mind-reading can clear you.
  • You cannot see whether the opponent was interfered with until the match is over.

6. Mind-reading

When struck, you can spend 1 point to read the opponent’s mind: the game re-evaluates this round with the opponent’s original brain.

With “burn OKB for each mind-read” ticked, each read also sends a transaction on X Layer that burns 0.0001 OKB to the 0x…dEaD address. That OKB is never refunded, the read only happens after the transaction confirms, and every read transaction is linked in the match log. It is the real price of transparency: in a future version both players stake OKB, the winner takes the pot, and mind-reading fees are part of the game.

  • If the result is Cooperate, it was the sophons: the opponent’s move is set back to Cooperate, the round is scored on real moves, and your fee is refunded.
  • If it is still Strike, that is the opponent’s nature: you lose 1 point and the score stands.

The opponent’s brain is secret during the match, so you have to guess: was that strike the sophons, or did you meet a Cleaner?
